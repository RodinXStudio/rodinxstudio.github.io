Pishooni · پیشونی

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how RodinX Studio handles information in the Pishooni (پیشونی) mobile game.

Information we collect

The current version of Pishooni does not require an account and does not collect, transmit, or store personal information on RodinX Studio servers. The game has no backend service, analytics service, advertising SDK, or in-app purchase system.

Information stored on your device

Pishooni may store game preferences and limited game-state/results information locally on your device so that settings and game features work as expected. This local information is not uploaded to RodinX Studio.

Device features and permissions

The Android version uses vibration for optional gameplay feedback. Motion sensors are used to detect phone movement during gameplay (for example, tilt gestures for correct answers or passes). Sensor readings are used for gameplay and are not sent to RodinX Studio.

Advertising and analytics

The current version of Pishooni does not display advertising and does not use analytics or tracking technologies.

Children's privacy

Pishooni does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users. The game can be played without creating an account or providing personal information.

Data sharing

RodinX Studio does not sell or share personal information collected through Pishooni because the current version of the game does not collect personal information.

Data retention and deletion

RodinX Studio does not retain user personal data from Pishooni on its servers. Locally stored game preferences and game-state/results information can be removed by clearing the app's local data or uninstalling the app.

Changes to this policy

If Pishooni's features or data practices change, this Privacy Policy may be updated. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact RodinX Studio at rodinxstudio [at] gmail [dot] com.
